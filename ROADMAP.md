NEXUS Project Roadmap 🌌


This roadmap outlines the planned development of NEXUS, an experimental platform for studying AI agent behaviour in persistent, multi-agent environments.


Current status: Early planning and research. The prototype has not yet been built.


Phase 1 — Research and Design




Define a focused first research question.


Review existing AI agent evaluation tools and research.


Decide what behaviours and events to record.


Choose a manageable technical approach.




Goal: A clear, testable plan before implementation begins.


Phase 2 — Build a Small Environment




Create a simple environment for a small number of AI agents.


Define the environment's rules and state.


Enable agents to interact.


Record actions, events and interactions.




Goal: A working experimental environment with observable behaviour.


Phase 3 — Record and Compare Experiments




Save experiment logs and relevant environment states.


Repeat experiments under defined conditions.


Compare runs where selected variables differ.


Document the experimental setup and results.




Goal: Make experiments easier to inspect, repeat and compare.


Phase 4 — Analyse and Evaluate




Examine what happened during each run.


Identify measurable behavioural differences.


Consider alternative explanations and limitations.


Document findings and unanswered questions.




Goal: Establish what the experiments demonstrate and what remains uncertain.


Collaboration


We welcome constructive input from Python developers, AI engineers, simulation specialists and people with experience in AI safety or evaluation research.


All phases are planned work. The roadmap may change as research, feedback and technical testing progress.


Principles




Test claims rather than assume results.


Prefer small, controlled experiments.


Keep evidence separate from conclusions.


Document limitations and unsuccessful experiments.


Build only what can be evaluated meaningfully.
