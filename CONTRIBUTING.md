Contributing to NEXUS 🌌


Thank you for your interest in NEXUS — The Living World.


NEXUS is an early-stage project exploring AI agent behaviour in persistent, multi-agent environments. We welcome thoughtful technical contributions, constructive feedback and research discussion.


Areas of Interest




Python and AI agent development


Simulation and environment design


Backend systems, APIs and data logging


Frontend development and data visualisation


AI safety and agent evaluation


Experimental design, testing and documentation




How to Get Involved




Read the project README and roadmap.


Review the open GitHub issues.


Open an issue to introduce yourself, describe your relevant experience and suggest how you would like to contribute.


Discuss the scope and approach before starting substantial work.


Agree on responsibilities, attribution, intellectual property and expectations before contributing.




Please do not post private contact details, credentials or confidential material in public issues.


Working Principles




Be respectful and constructive.


Prefer small, clearly scoped tasks.


Explain technical decisions and assumptions.


Document tests, results and limitations.


Do not present planned features as completed work.




Voluntary Contributions


NEXUS is currently an early-stage project without secured funding for contributor payments. Contributions are voluntary unless different written terms are agreed in advance.


There is no guarantee of employment, equity, publication or future financial returns. Intellectual property ownership, licensing, attribution and permitted use of contributions must be discussed and agreed before work begins.


Research Integrity


The project aims to investigate agent behaviour rigorously. We welcome criticism, reproducible experiments and evidence that challenges our assumptions.


Thank you for helping us explore the possibilities of AI agent evaluation.
