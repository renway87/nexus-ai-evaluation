# nexus ai evaluation
NEXUS The Living World 🌌


Exploring AI Agent Behaviour in Persistent Environments


What happens when AI agents don't just complete a task, but live, interact, remember and make decisions over time?


NEXUS is an early stage AI agent evaluation project exploring how agents behave in persistent, multi-agent environments.


The vision is to create a platform where AI agents can operate within an evolving world, interact with other agents, respond to changing circumstances and build up a history of interactions  while their behaviour is recorded for analysis.


Our goal is to investigate whether long running, interactive environments can reveal useful insights about AI behaviour that shorter or more static evaluations might miss.


🔬 What We're Exploring




Persistent environments: Worlds that evolve as agents act.


Multi-agent interaction: Cooperation, communication, conflict and coordination.


Memory and relationships: How interaction history may influence later decisions.


Behavioural observation: Recording actions, events, messages and changes in the environment.


Controlled experiments: Comparing different runs to investigate why behaviour changes.


AI evaluation: Turning observed behaviour into evidence that researchers and developers can inspect.




These are research and development goals. The platform is at an early stage, and its capabilities and research value still need to be tested.


🚀 Our First Milestone


We're working towards a small prototype that can:




Run a small number of AI agents in a persistent environment.


Record their actions and interactions.


Save checkpoints and compare experimental runs.


Present the resulting evidence in a clear, inspectable format.




The priority is to build a working foundation, test specific research questions and learn from measurable results.
